English Grammar for Students

Future Simple & Verb To Be (Present Simple)

Future Simple Verb To Be Exercises

1. Future Simple (Will)

The future simple is used to talk about actions that will happen in the future.

Form:

Affirmative: Subject + will + verb

I will study English.

Negative: Subject + will not (won't) + verb

I won't study today.

Question: Will + subject + verb?

Will you study English?

Uses:

  • Decisions made now
  • Promises
  • Predictions

2. Verb To Be (Present Simple)

The verb to be is used to describe states, identity, and situations.

Forms:

I am a student.

You are my friend.

He / She / It is happy.

We are ready.

They are here.

Negative:

I am not tired.

She is not at home.

Questions:

Am I late?

Is he your brother?

Are they students?
