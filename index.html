Future Simple & Verb To Be (Present Simple)

Future Simple Verb To Be Exercises

1. Future Simple (Will)

The future simple is used to talk about actions that will happen in the future.

Form:

Affirmative: Subject + will + base verb

I will study English.

Negative: Subject + will not (won't) + base verb

I won't study today.

Questions: Will + subject + base verb?

Will you study English?

Common Uses:

  • Making spontaneous decisions ("I'll answer the phone!")
  • Making promises or offers ("I will help you.")
  • Making predictions ("It will rain tomorrow.")

2. Verb "To Be" (Present Simple)

The verb to be describes states, identity, or situations in the present.

Positive Forms:

I am a student.

You are my friend.

He / She / It is happy.

We are ready.

They are here.

Negative Forms:

I am not tired. → I'm not tired.

She is not at home. → She isn't at home.

Questions:

Am I late?

Is he your brother?

Are they students?
